Who we are

Started by students, carried by students

Living Waters Incorporated began as a small group of secondary school students who wanted their gifts in music and movement to serve something bigger than the stage. What started as after-school rehearsals grew into two distinct wings — the Living Waters Singers and the Living Waters Dancers — now invited to schools, camps and churches to lead worship, perform and share the Gospel through testimony.

What we do

Two wings, one mission

Living Waters Singers

Voices trained for worship and witness

The Singers lead congregational worship, perform original and cover pieces, and use music as an entry point into schools that might never open their doors to a straightforward sermon.

Living Waters Dancers

Choreography with a message

The Dancers tell Bible stories and life testimonies through movement — routines built to hold a school assembly's attention long enough to deliver a message of hope.
