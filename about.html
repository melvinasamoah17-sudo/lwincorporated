About Living Waters Incorporated

Faith That's Seen And Heard

Living Waters Incorporated is a student-led movement raising up young people in secondary schools who want their faith to be seen and heard — not just spoken. Born out of a desire to give students a creative outlet for worship and expression, the group brings together two vibrant wings: the Living Waters Singers and the Living Waters Dancers.

The Living Waters Singers lend their voices to lead worship, minister through song, and inspire their peers with music that speaks to the heart. The Living Waters Dancers bring that same spirit to life through movement — using dance as a language of praise, storytelling, and celebration.

Together, these two groups form the creative heartbeat of Living Waters Incorporated, giving students a place to grow their gifts, build community, and lead others — both in their schools and beyond. Whether on stage or behind the scenes, every member plays a part in a shared mission: to let their light — and their talent — shine.

"We are a generation raised to worship"

Our Mission

Discipling Students Through Song And Dance

Working with school chaplains, Christian fellowships and churches, Living Waters Incorporated exists to make the Gospel unmissable to students — using music and choreography as a bridge into schools and communities that a straightforward sermon might not reach, and pointing every student who watches us toward a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

Christ-Centered Worship

Every song and routine begins and ends in prayer. The performance is the platform; Christ is the point.

Excellence In Craft

We train seriously in vocal technique, harmony and choreography, because a message deserves a worthy delivery.

Discipline & Teamwork

Rehearsals build character as much as skill — punctuality, humility and looking out for one another on and off stage.

Boldness In Witness

We go where we're invited, including rooms that don't always expect a Gospel message, and we don't shrink back.

Servant Leadership

Older members mentor younger ones. Every student leader here first learned to serve before they learned to lead.

Why Join

What Students Gain By Joining

A Place To Belong

A supportive community of peers who share the same faith and passion for creative expression.

Room To Grow Their Talent

Hands-on training and regular practice in singing or dance, whatever their gift.

Leadership Experience

Opportunities to take on roles within the group's student leadership structure and develop real organisational and people skills.

A Platform To Perform And Minister

Chances to sing, dance, and lead worship in front of their schools and beyond.

Confidence And Character

Discipline, teamwork, and public performance experience that carries into every other part of school life.

A space to deepen their faith alongside others walking the same journey.
